# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in SignalForge respectful,
constructive, and welcoming regardless of background or identity.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Give actionable technical feedback.
- Keep discussions focused on the work and its impact.
- Protect private information and report security issues responsibly.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, doxxing, sexualized
conduct, and deliberate exposure of private or security-sensitive information
are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report concerns to the repository maintainers through the private security or
project contact channel. Maintainers may remove content, request changes, or
restrict participation when needed to keep the project safe and constructive.
